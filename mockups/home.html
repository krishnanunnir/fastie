Kino home · three directions, TV and phone

A · Marquee

The direction DESIGN.md already describes, finished properly. A near-black ground, the focused title's backdrop behind deeper scrims, white as the only focus colour, and amber only for progress, the bolt and the current page.

  • Focus ring with a gap: the white outline sits 2dp off the poster, and posters without focus dim slightly, so the focused one is easy to spot from across the room.
  • Rail opens on focus: press ← from the first poster and the rail widens to show labels, the way big-screen apps do it.
  • Bigger title art, a meta line with a rating badge, and a three-layer scrim so the type stays readable over bright art.

Code cost: small. Mostly the focus drawable in Posters.kt, the scrim in Ui.kt and the rail expand in MainActivity.kt. It means rolling back the Material You colours in your uncommitted changes.

B · Tonal

Material 3 Expressive with dynamic colour, which is where your uncommitted code is heading. Surfaces take a warm tint from the wallpaper on Android 12 and later, with amber as the fallback seed. The hero becomes a rounded card, and navigation uses pill indicators.

  • Continue Watching as wide cards with the episode and time left under each one, so you can tell what you're resuming without opening it.
  • Meta shown as tonal chips. Play is a filled pill and My List a tonal pill. Posters get softer 14dp corners.
  • Focus is a primary-colour ring with a gap. It feels native next to Google TV and Pixel apps.

Code cost: medium. It finishes the work in progress (C tokens, Shape, SPRING), adds a landscape card type to Posters.kt, and DESIGN.md gets rewritten around it.

C · Spotlight

The most cinematic of the three. On TV the focused title's backdrop fills the whole screen, the rail is replaced by text tabs along the top so posters get the full width, and the focused poster widens into a 16:9 card showing its own art.

  • Phone hero as a floating poster on a glow tinted from the artwork, with Play and My List built into the card.
  • Continue Watching uses landscape stills with the episode on them, and the rows sit lower and quieter.
  • True-black ground, with amber kept to progress, the wordmark and the active tab.

Code cost: medium to high. It needs a width animation on the focused RecyclerView item, a top tab bar for TV, and an average colour from each poster (scaling the bitmap to 1×1 is enough, no new library).
