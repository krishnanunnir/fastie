# Home and browse (TV-first, phone too)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Stremio/Nuvio addon users on the couch with a remote. Job: find something and start it in three steps (title → stream → play).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The poster you're on is the whole screen. The focused title's backdrop, logo and synopsis own the top 55%, and rows of posters sit below. Refuses the grid-of-cards dashboard and the settings-heavy "media center" look.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black living-room ground (#0F0F11), white type, Netflix grey (#A3A3A8) for secondary text, one accent, Fastie amber (#FFB627), only on progress bars, the wordmark and the active rail item. 2:3 posters with 6dp corners, no borders at rest. Focus is a 1.08 scale plus a 3dp white outline. System sans in bold/medium/regular steps.

STORY: Open the app → the first row is already focused → move around and the backdrop crossfades to whatever you're on → OK opens details → Play → streams grouped by addon → playback.

FIRST VIEWPORT: TV: a slim left rail (search, home, my list, settings icons). Backdrop full-bleed, darkened by left and bottom scrims. Title logo (or bold title) top-left at 40% width, meta line (year · genres · ★ rating), 3-line synopsis. Rows begin at 58% height, with the first row's posters fully visible. Phone: the same hero, static on the first item with Play and My List buttons; rows below; bottom navigation bar.

FORM: Category canon played straight (the user's standing exit), Netflix TV as the bar. seed: none (pinned by brief). concept-seed was not run. new-work.md §4: "The user may re-roll freely, and a user- or brief-pinned direction beats the roll, always."
